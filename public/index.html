FAQs

Do you have more questions?

What is CDP and how does it work? +

CDP is a protocol that lets crypto tokens distribute revenue to their holders. Just by holding a supported token, you automatically start earning a share of the rewards accumulated by the project.

Do I need to stake to earn rewards? +

No. Rewards accumulate automatically while you hold tokens. But to unlock and use your rewards, you'll need to stake your tokens.

What happens when I stake my tokens? +

Staking gives you access to 90% of your earned rewards. Your tokens are held by the protocol while staked – you can get them back by unstaking.

Can I unstake without returning the rewards? +

Yes, sometimes. If more rewards were added after you staked, you can often unstake part of your tokens without returning anything. To fully unstake, just return the rewards you claimed.

How are rewards calculated? +

Rewards are based on your share of the total token supply and the amount of value the project distributes to the protocol.

Do I need to use a special wallet? +

No. Any Ethereum-compatible wallet works. Just hold the supported token to start accumulating rewards.

How do I know if a project is legit? +

Projects listed on the platform go through a verification process and are approved by our DAO community. Only verified, yield-generating projects are shown.

Where can I track my earnings? +

You'll have access to a dashboard showing your holdings, accumulated rewards, staking status, and full history.
